# How to Restart Your Router

Restarting your router can help resolve internet connectivity issues. This simple process can refresh your network settings and improve performance.

1. **Unplug the Router:** Carefully unplug the power cable from your router.
2. **Wait for a Minute:** Allow the router to cool down for about a minute.
3. **Plug it Back In:** Plug the power cable back into the router and wait for it to fully restart.
4. **Check Internet Connection:** Connect to the internet and check if the issue is resolved.

If your internet still isn’t working after following these steps, it may be necessary to contact support.

---

**When to Contact Support:**
- If the issue persists after the router restarts.
- If you are unsure about the steps.
- If you need assistance with setting up your router.
