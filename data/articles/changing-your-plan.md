# Changing your plan
Quickly switch to a different plan with just a few steps.

1. **Log in to your account**: Visit the website and enter your username and password to access your account dashboard.
2. **Navigate to Plans section**: Look for a menu or sidebar and click on "Plans" or "Subscription."
3. **Select a new plan**: Choose the plan that best fits your needs from the available options.
4. **Follow on-screen instructions**: Complete the process by following any additional prompts or requirements, such as confirming the change or entering payment details if necessary.
5. **Review and confirm**: Double-check that the new plan is selected and that all details are correct before finalizing the change.

**When to contact support**:
- If you encounter any errors during the process.
- If you have questions about the features or costs of the plans.
- If you need assistance with payment methods or billing issues.
