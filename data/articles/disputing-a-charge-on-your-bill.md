# Disputing a Charge on Your Bill

Learn how to dispute a charge on your telecom bill easily and quickly.

1. **Review your bill carefully**: Check the date, service, and amount in dispute.
2. **Contact your customer service**: Reach out to the support line or use online chat during business hours.
3. **Provide proof of the charge**: Have the exact details ready, such as the service date and description.
4. **Explain the issue**: Clearly state why you believe the charge is incorrect.
5. **Follow up**: Ensure you receive a confirmation of the dispute and expected resolution time.

**When to contact support**: If the issue is not resolved within a week, or if you need further assistance.

Remember, timely communication is key to a swift resolution.
