# Upgrading to 5G

Upgrading to 5G is simple and offers faster speeds and improved connectivity. Follow these steps to ensure a smooth transition.

1. **Check Compatibility**: Ensure your device supports 5G. Visit our website for a list of compatible devices.
2. **Update Your Plan**: Contact customer service to check if your current plan supports 5G. Upgrade your plan if necessary.
3. **Restart Your Device**: Sometimes a simple restart can resolve 5G connection issues.
4. **Move to a Suitable Location**: Ensure you're in an area with 5G coverage. Check our website for 5G coverage maps.
5. **Update Software and Apps**: Make sure your device and apps are up to date for optimal 5G performance.

## When to Contact Support

- If you encounter ongoing issues with your 5G connection.
- If you do not see any 5G signal in your area.
- If your 5G speeds are significantly lower than expected.

For assistance, visit our support portal or contact our help desk.
