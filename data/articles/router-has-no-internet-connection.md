# Router has no internet connection

Your router is an essential piece of equipment for maintaining internet access in your home or office. If you are experiencing no internet connection, follow these steps to resolve the issue.

1. **Check the Power and Reset the Router:**
   - Ensure that the router is plugged into a power source and turned on.
   - Disconnect the power from the router for about 30 seconds, then reconnect it.

2. **Verify Connection to Modem:**
   - Ensure that your router is properly connected to the modem. Use the correct Ethernet cable and check that it is securely plugged into both the router and the modem.

3. **Check the Internet Connection on Your Device:**
   - Try accessing the internet using a different device or browser to confirm that the issue is with the router and not your device.

4. **Access Router Settings:**
   - Connect to your router’s Wi-Fi network and open a web browser to the router’s IP address (commonly 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1).
   - Log in to your router’s settings and look for any error messages or warnings.

5. **Restart Your Modem:**
   - If the problem persists, try disconnecting the power from the modem for about 30 seconds and then reconnecting it.

6. **Check with Your Internet Service Provider:**
   - If none of the above steps resolve the issue, your internet service provider may have a maintenance issue. Contact them to confirm.

**When to Contact Support:**
If you have followed these steps and your router still has no internet connection, it may be necessary to contact technical support.
