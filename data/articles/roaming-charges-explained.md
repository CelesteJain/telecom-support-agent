# Roaming charges explained

Traveling abroad can sometimes lead to unexpected charges on your phone bill. Understanding roaming charges can help you manage your expenses.

## Troubleshooting steps:

1. **Check your plan**: Ensure your plan allows for international roaming or consider purchasing a roaming package before traveling.
2. **Turn off automatic data roaming**: This can save you from unexpected charges. Go to your phone settings and turn off data roaming when not needed.
3. **Monitor your data usage**: Keep an eye on your data consumption to avoid accidental use that could lead to extra charges.
4. **Use local Wi-Fi**: Where possible, connect to free Wi-Fi to avoid using mobile data.

## When to contact support:

- If you notice charges that do not match your usage.
- If you cannot find a way to reduce your roaming charges.
- If you have any questions about your roaming plan or usage.

By following these steps, you can better manage your roaming expenses and enjoy your travels without unnecessary financial worries.
