# Changing your wifi password

Change your home network's password easily in a few simple steps.

1. **Log in to your router's settings**: Connect to your router's interface via a web browser. Type your router’s IP address in the address bar.
2. **Navigate to the security settings**: Look for a section called "Wireless Security" or "Wireless Settings."
3. **Update your password**: Find the option to change the password and enter your new password.
4. **Save your changes**: Make sure to save the new password to apply the changes.

If you encounter issues like not being able to log in to your router or saving changes, please contact support.

When to contact support:
- If you're unable to access your router's settings page.
- If you receive error messages after saving your changes.
- If you need assistance with forgotten login credentials.
