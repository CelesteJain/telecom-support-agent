# Understanding Your Bill

A clear understanding of your bill can help you manage your services effectively. This guide will help you interpret the different components of your bill.

1. **Review the Service Summary**: At the top of your bill, you'll find a summary of the services you’re using, including any added services like international calling or messaging.
2. **Check the Charges**: Below the summary, you’ll see a list of charges. Each charge is broken down into service fees, data usage, and any other specific costs. If a charge seems incorrect, check the next step.
3. **Verify Your Data Usage**: If you’re unsure about high data charges, log into your account online or use the mobile app to view your data usage details. This can help you understand if you went over your plan or if there was a billing error.
4. **Look for Promotions or Discounts**: Some bills include promotions or discounts that you can use to save on future bills.

When to Contact Support:
- If you find discrepancies that you cannot resolve, contact customer support.
- If you have questions about specific charges or services.
- If your bill does not arrive on time or is missing information.
