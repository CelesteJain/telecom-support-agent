# Activating a New SIM
Quickly activate your new SIM card with these easy steps.

1. **Insert the SIM card into your device**: Ensure your device is turned off to avoid any interference during insertion. Carefully align the SIM card with the slot and press gently until it clicks into place.
2. **Turn on your device**: Power up your phone or device to allow it to recognize the new SIM card.
3. **Check the activation status**: Go to your device's settings and navigate to 'SIM card & mobile networks.' If your SIM card is not activated, you may see a message indicating that it needs to be activated.
4. **Enter the activation code**: If prompted, enter the activation code provided with your SIM card. This code is usually a short alphanumeric sequence.

### When to Contact Support
- If your device does not detect the SIM card after following the above steps.
- If you do not receive an activation code or if the code is invalid.
- If you encounter any other issues during the activation process.

Remember, activation codes are valid only once, so use them carefully.
