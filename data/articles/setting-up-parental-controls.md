# Setting up Parental Controls

Ensure your family's online safety with our easy-to-follow parental control setup guide.

1. **Check Your Device Compatibility** - Ensure your device supports parental controls.
2. **Log In to Your Account** - Access your account on our website or mobile app.
3. **Navigate to Settings** - Look for the settings menu and select "Parental Controls."
4. **Create a New Profile** - Enter the profile name and select age limits or specific apps/games to restrict.
5. **Set Time Limits** - Configure the time of day when usage is allowed or restricted.
6. **Save Changes** - Confirm and save your settings.

**When to Contact Support:**
- If you encounter errors during setup.
- If you need assistance with advanced features.
- If you have questions about specific content filtering options.
