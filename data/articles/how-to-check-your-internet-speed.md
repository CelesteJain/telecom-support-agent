# How to Check Your Internet Speed

A quick guide to measure your internet speed and ensure it meets your plan.

1. **Use a Reliable Speed Test Tool**: Visit a reputable website like SpeedTest by Ookla or a similar service to run a speed test. Make sure your device is connected to the internet and follow the on-screen instructions.
2. **Close Unnecessary Applications**: Before starting the test, close any applications that may be using internet bandwidth, such as streaming services or download programs. This helps ensure accurate results.
3. **Restart Your Router**: Occasionally, a simple restart of your router can improve its performance and provide more accurate speed test results. Unplug the router for at least 30 seconds, then plug it back in and wait for it to fully restart.
4. **Check During Off-Peak Hours**: Run your speed test during times when the internet is less congested, such as late at night or early in the morning. This can help determine if speed issues are related to high usage times.

## When to Contact Support

If your speed consistently falls below the promised level after trying these steps, or if you experience other issues like frequent disconnections or buffering, contact customer support for further assistance.
