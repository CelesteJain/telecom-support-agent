# SIM Card Not Working
SIM card issues can cause your device to lose connectivity. Follow these steps to resolve the problem.

1. **Check SIM Card and Device**: Ensure your SIM card is properly inserted and not damaged. Try removing and reinserting the SIM card.
2. **Restart Your Device**: Turn off your device, wait for 30 seconds, and then turn it back on.
3. **Try Another SIM Card**: Insert a different SIM card to see if the issue persists. This can help identify if the problem is with your SIM card or device.
4. **Check Signal Strength**: Ensure your device is in an area with good network coverage. Move to a different location if possible.
5. **Update Device Software**: Check for and install the latest software updates for your device.

If the issue continues, it may be necessary to contact support.

**When to contact support**:
- If you have tried all troubleshooting steps.
- If the issue started after a recent software update.
- If you are unable to connect to any network, including Wi-Fi.
