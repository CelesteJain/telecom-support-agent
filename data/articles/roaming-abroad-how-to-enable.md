# Roaming abroad: How to enable

Enable roaming services to make calls, send texts, and use data when traveling internationally.

1. **Check your plan:** Ensure your current plan includes international roaming. If not, contact customer service to activate it.
2. **Go to settings:** Open your phone's settings and navigate to 'Network & internet' or 'Connections'.
3. **Enable roaming:** Find the 'Roaming' or 'International Access' option and turn it on.
4. **Save settings:** Some phones require you to save changes or restart the device for changes to take effect.

**When to contact support:**
- If you cannot find the roaming settings.
- If your phone displays a 'No Service' or 'Roaming' message.
- If your roaming settings do not work as expected during your travels.
