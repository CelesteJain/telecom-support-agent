# Text messages not sending

This guide will help you resolve issues with text messages not being sent from your device.

1. **Check your internet connection**: Ensure that your device is connected to the internet. If using Wi-Fi, make sure it’s stable and active.
2. **Restart your device**: Turn off your phone and turn it back on. This can resolve temporary software glitches.
3. **Check your message settings**: Go to your phone's settings and ensure that text messaging is enabled. Also, check if there are any network or service settings that might be affecting text messages.
4. **Clear cache and data**: On your device, go to settings > apps > [Your Telecom App] > storage > clear cache and data. Be cautious when doing this as it may remove some app data.
5. **Update your app**: Ensure that your telecom app is up to date. Check for updates in the app store or through the app itself.

If you have tried the above steps and still face issues, it may be best to contact our support team.

**When to contact support**: If the problem persists after trying these steps, or if you encounter any errors during the troubleshooting process.
