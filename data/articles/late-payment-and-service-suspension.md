# Late Payment and Service Suspension

If you are facing service suspension due to late payment, follow these steps to resolve the issue quickly and get your service back online.

1. **Check Your Payment Status**: Log in to your account online or via the mobile app to view your payment status and due date.
2. **Make the Payment**: Pay your outstanding balance as soon as possible. You can pay online, visit a nearby payment center, or use a mobile payment app.
3. **Verify Payment**: Ensure the payment has been successfully processed by checking your account again or contacting customer service for confirmation.
4. **Resume Service**: Once your payment is confirmed, your service should be automatically restored. If not, check your account settings or contact customer service.

## When to Contact Support

- If you find discrepancies in your bill.
- If your service does not resume after making the payment.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with the payment process.

Remember, timely payment can help avoid service suspension and maintain uninterrupted service.
