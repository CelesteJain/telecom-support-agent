# Broadband is slower than expected

Learn how to diagnose and fix common issues that may be causing your broadband speeds to be slower than expected.

1. **Check your device settings**: Ensure that your computer, tablet, or smartphone is set to the correct network. Disconnect and reconnect to the internet to refresh the connection.
2. **Restart your modem and router**: Turn off your modem and router, wait for one minute, and then power them back on. This can resolve temporary issues and optimize performance.
3. **Verify your plan and speed**: Log in to your account on the provider's website or app to check your current service plan and any speed limits. Confirm that your subscription matches your expected speeds.
4. **Run a speed test**: Use an independent speed test website or app to measure your internet speed. Compare the results with the speeds promised by your provider.
5. **Update your software and drivers**: Ensure that your device's operating system and network drivers are up to date. Software updates can often resolve compatibility issues that affect speed.

### When to contact support

If after following these steps your broadband speeds remain slower than expected, or if you encounter any other issues, contact customer support. Our team can help diagnose more complex problems and provide further assistance.
