# Moving Home and Transferring Service

Moving to a new address and ensuring your telecom service is transferred seamlessly can be a hassle. Follow these steps to make the process smoother.

1. **Check Service Eligibility:** Before you move, confirm that your current service is eligible for transfer. Not all services can be moved to a new location.
2. **Schedule Your Move Date:** Inform your telecom provider of your move date at least 30 days in advance. This allows sufficient time to process your request.
3. **Update Contact Information:** Ensure your new address and contact details are updated with your provider. This prevents any delays in service.
4. **Transfer Service:** Visit your provider’s website or contact them directly to start the service transfer process. Provide all necessary documentation, such as your new address proof and ID.
5. **Confirm Service Activation:** Upon moving in, contact your provider to confirm that the service has been activated at your new address.

**When to Contact Support:**  
If you encounter any issues during the transfer process, or if your service is not activated as scheduled, contact customer support for assistance.

---

This guide is designed to help you smoothly transition your telecom service to your new home.
