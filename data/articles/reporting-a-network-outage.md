# Reporting a Network Outage
Quickly inform our technical team of any network issues to ensure a speedy resolution.

1. **Check if it’s a widespread issue:** Visit our status page to see if others are experiencing similar problems. This can save time as the issue may already be known and being addressed.
2. **Verify your connection:** Restart your modem and router to rule out any local device-related problems. Wait for a few minutes to see if your service returns.
3. **Access the status page:** Visit our website’s status page to report your issue or check for updates on the outage.
4. **Contact us if the problem persists:** If your service remains down, you can report the issue by clicking the "Report an Outage" button on the status page or by using the live chat feature.

**When to contact support:**  
- If you've tried troubleshooting steps and the issue persists.
- If you notice data loss or critical services are affected.
- If the outage lasts longer than 24 hours or if you're experiencing intermittent service issues that affect your daily activities.

By following these steps, you can help us quickly identify and resolve the issue, minimizing the impact on your service.
