# Router Lights Explained

A quick guide to understanding your router's light indicators for easy troubleshooting.

1. **Check Power and Connection**: Ensure the power light is on and that your modem or other devices are properly connected to the router via Ethernet cables.
2. **Verify Internet Connection**: Look for the internet or WAN light. If it's not blinking or is solid, there might be an issue with your internet service provider.
3. **Check Wireless Connectivity**: If the wireless light is not on or is blinking slowly, your Wi-Fi might be experiencing problems. Restart the router or check the router’s settings.
4. **Network Activity**: A steady or blinking LAN light indicates activity on your local network, while a solid or steady light suggests no activity.
5. **Red or Amber Lights**: These typically indicate an error or problem that needs attention, such as a configuration issue or a device not connected correctly.

## When to Contact Support
If after checking the above steps, your router’s lights are still not behaving as expected, or if you are experiencing consistent internet connectivity issues, it’s best to contact our support team for further assistance.
