# Voicemail Setup and Problems

Voicemail allows you to receive messages when you can't answer your calls. Here’s how to set it up and troubleshoot common issues.

1. **Setting Up Voicemail:**
   - Log in to your account dashboard or use the mobile app.
   - Navigate to the 'Voicemail' section.
   - Follow the on-screen instructions to create a voicemail password and set your greeting.

2. **Troubleshooting Common Issues:**
   - **Issue: Voicemail not working.**
     - Check if voicemail is enabled on your account.
     - Verify that your voicemail password is correct.
   - **Issue: Unable to access voicemail messages.**
     - Ensure you have the correct password.
     - Check your phone settings to ensure voicemail is set to ring through to your device.
   - **Issue: Voicemail greeting sounds off.**
     - Re-record your greeting in the app.
     - Make sure the file is in a supported format (MP3, WAV).

3. **When to Contact Support:**
   - If you continue to experience issues after following the steps.
   - If you need assistance with transferring voicemail messages to another device.
   - For any questions regarding voicemail service plans or features.

Remember, your voicemail is an important tool for communication. If you need further assistance, our support team is here to help.
