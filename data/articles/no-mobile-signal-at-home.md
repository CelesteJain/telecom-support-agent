# No mobile signal at home

Mobile signal issues at home can be frustrating. Follow these steps to resolve the problem.

1. **Check the Area**: Ensure your location is not in a remote or rural area where coverage is limited.
2. **Move Closer to the Window**: Sometimes, moving closer to a window or outside can improve signal strength.
3. **Restart Your Device**: Turn off and on your mobile device to refresh its connection.
4. **Update Your Software**: Ensure your device’s software is up to date to avoid compatibility issues.
5. **Test with Another Device**: Use a different mobile device to see if the issue is specific to one device.
6. **Check for Interference**: Move away from devices that might be interfering with your signal, such as microwaves or routers.

If you have tried these steps and still experience no mobile signal at home, it may be time to contact customer support.

**When to contact support**:
- If the issue persists after all troubleshooting steps.
- If you suspect a hardware issue with your device.
- If you need technical assistance beyond these basic steps.
