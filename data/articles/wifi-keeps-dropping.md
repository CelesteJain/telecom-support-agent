# Wifi Keeps Dropping
Troubleshoot common issues that may cause your Wi-Fi connection to drop frequently.

1. **Restart Your Devices**: Turn off and then turn on your router and any devices that connect to the internet, including smartphones and laptops.
2. **Check Physical Interference**: Ensure that there are no physical obstacles, such as walls or metal objects, that might be interfering with the Wi-Fi signal.
3. **Update Firmware**: Make sure your router’s firmware is up to date. Check your router’s settings for any firmware update options.
4. **Reduce Device Usage**: Limit the number of devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network to reduce the load on your router.
5. **Change Your Router’s Channel**: Sometimes, using a different Wi-Fi channel can improve signal strength and reduce interference from neighboring networks.

### When to Contact Support
If your Wi-Fi issues persist after trying these steps, consider contacting customer support for further assistance.
