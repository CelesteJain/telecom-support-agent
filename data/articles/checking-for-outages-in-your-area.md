# Checking for outages in your area
Stay informed about service disruptions in your neighborhood. This guide helps you quickly check for outages.

1. **Visit the Telecom Company’s Official Website:** Go to the company’s website and navigate to the customer support section. Most websites have a dedicated page for checking outages.
2. **Use the Outage Map Feature:** Look for an interactive map or a section that highlights areas experiencing service disruptions. This map often updates in real-time.
3. **Check the Mobile App:** If you have downloaded the company’s mobile app, check if it offers a service disruption feature or an outage map.
4. **Follow Social Media Channels:** Many telecom companies use social media to alert customers about outages and estimated restoration times.

**When to contact support:** If the outage map does not show any issues in your area but you are still experiencing service problems, or if you need more detailed information on the status of outages not listed.

By following these steps, you can stay informed and quickly address any service disruptions affecting your area.
