# Replacing a Lost or Damaged SIM
Replacements are quick and easy with these simple steps. Follow our guide to get back online.

1. **Check if you have a spare SIM card.** If you do, insert it into your device to ensure your service isn't completely lost.
2. **Log in to your account.** Visit [YourAccountPortal.com](http://YourAccountPortal.com) and select the option to replace your SIM.
3. **Select your device type.** Choose the device that your SIM is for from the drop-down menu.
4. **Follow the prompts to order a new SIM card.** This usually involves providing your account details and shipping information.
5. **Wait for your new SIM card.** It will be shipped to you within 3-5 business days.
6. **Activate your new SIM card.** Once you receive it, follow the instructions provided to activate your new SIM card.

If you encounter issues or need further assistance, contact our support team at [SupportPortal.com](http://SupportPortal.com).
