# Transferring your number to another provider

Transferring your phone number to a new provider is straightforward, but there are a few steps you need to follow to ensure a smooth transition.

1. **Check Eligibility**: Before starting, verify if your number is eligible for porting. This usually depends on how long you have been with your current provider.
2. **Contact Your Current Provider**: Request an authorization code for number porting. They will provide you with a unique code that you can use during the transfer process.
3. **Choose a New Provider**: Select the new telecom provider and prepare to transfer your number. Ensure the new provider supports number porting.
4. **Submit the Code**: When prompted, enter the authorization code provided by your current provider.
5. **Complete the Transfer**: Follow the new provider's instructions to finalize the transfer. This may include setting up your account and services.

When to contact support:
- If you receive an error message or encounter a problem during the transfer process.
- If you need assistance in finding an eligible provider that supports number porting.
- If you have been unable to contact your current provider for an authorization code.
