# Cannot make or receive calls

2. **Restart Your Device:** A simple restart can often resolve connectivity issues.

3. **Check Signal Strength:** If you're in a weak signal area, try moving to a better spot or using a cellular booster.

4. **Update Your Software:** Make sure your device and apps are up to date. Outdated software can cause call issues.

5. **Reset Network Settings:** This can fix issues related to your network settings. Be cautious as this will erase some network-specific data.

6. **Try a Different SIM Card:** If you have a backup SIM card, swap it with your current one to see if the issue persists.

7. **Factory Reset:** As a last resort, perform a factory reset. This will erase all data on your device, so back up important information first.

**When to Contact Support:** If none of the above steps work, or if you continue to experience issues, contact customer support for further assistance.
