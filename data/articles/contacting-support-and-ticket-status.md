# Contacting Support and Ticket Status

## One-Line Summary
Learn how to open a support ticket, check its status, and escalate issues efficiently.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. **Access Support Portal**: Log in to your account on our support portal using your username and password.
2. **Open a New Ticket**: Navigate to the “Open a Ticket” section. Fill out the required fields with detailed information about your issue.
3. **Check Ticket Status**: Once your ticket is submitted, use the “My Tickets” section to view the status updates. You can track the progress and receive notifications.
4. **Update Your Ticket**: If you need to add more information or update your ticket, you can do so directly through the portal.
5. **Escalate if Necessary**: If your issue is not resolved within the expected timeframe, use the “Escalate” option to get higher-level support.

## When to Contact Support
- When you encounter issues with your service that are not resolved by following the troubleshooting steps in our knowledge base.
- If you need assistance with account management, billing, or service changes.
- For urgent issues that require immediate attention, such as service outages or critical security concerns.
